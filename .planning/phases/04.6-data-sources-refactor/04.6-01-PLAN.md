<objective>
Replace the TMDB-only provider fetch (Phase 04-04) with a 3-layer cross-reference resolver:
1. **Curated per-region list** (locked in REGION-PLATFORMS-DRAFT.md) — primary source of truth, defines presence + display order + preSelected flags.
2. **Streaming-Availability (RapidAPI)** — freshness check; intersect curated with what SA reports as currently live for the country/type.
3. **TMDB** — last-resort fallback when both curated entry and SA entry are missing.

Activate the dormant `api/streaming-availability.js` proxy, add cost-aware caching (Vercel Runtime Cache, 24h TTL), and unify the onboarding S2 grid and the detail-page "Where to watch" overlay on the same resolver.

Purpose: Phase 04-04 r1-r7 surfaced TMDB cross-region contamination (Epix in TR, niche bleed in non-TR regions). Curated lists already locked; this plan wires them into the data layer.

Output: `src/data/region-platforms.js` (data), `src/lib/providers-resolver.js` (logic), `src/lib/sa-cache.js` (cache), wired SA proxy, logo asset set, and tests.
</objective>

<assumptions_locked>
- **A1:** Curated list is authoritative. SA acts as a freshness gate, not a discovery mechanism. If a platform is in the curated list but SA says it's not live in that country, the resolver still shows it (curated wins — SA data is patchy in non-EN regions).
- **A2:** SA "absent" ≠ "dead". Only treat SA 200 with explicit `not_available` as authoritative. 429/5xx/timeout → ignore SA layer for that request.
- **A3:** Cache key is `sa:providers:{country}:{type}` where type ∈ {movie, series}. TTL 24h. Stale-while-revalidate if @vercel/functions runtime-cache exposes it; if not, plain TTL.
