<objective>
Replace the current pure-Gemini trivia call (src/features/detail.js#loadTrivia, ~line 643) which hallucinates plausible-but-wrong facts (awards, dates, cast). The new flow:

1. **Fetch in parallel**: TMDB detail (already loaded) + OMDB lookup via IMDb ID (from TMDB external_ids).
2. **Build a structured ground-truth bundle**: `{ awards, plot, ratings:{rt,metacritic,imdb}, director, writer, runtime }` from OMDB + `{ keywords, cast:top5, crew:keyRoles, production_companies, tagline }` from TMDB.
3. **Build a grounded Gemini prompt** that embeds the bundle and instructs the model: "Use ONLY these facts. If unsure, say 'no notable trivia available.' Never invent awards, dates, names, or events. ≤3 short bullets."
4. **Fallback**: OMDB empty/error → TMDB-only grounding (still factual).
5. **Attribution**: micro-credit "Source: OMDB + TMDB" below the trivia block.

Purpose: App Store review risk + brand damage risk from fabricated facts. Lumi cannot ship a movie discovery app whose trivia invents Oscars that didn't happen.

Output: `src/lib/trivia-grounding.js`, refactored `loadTrivia()`, hardened `api/omdb.js`, and prompt-content tests.
</objective>
